# Stage 11 statistical analysis plan

Recorded before feature-level testing. Two-sided null for each eligible measurement: the adjusted AD minus Control coefficient is zero. AD and Control are the only included original diagnosis labels. Run each cohort separately. The external cohort is locked.

- Verify IDs, matrix alignment, eligible mapping class and exact source values against Stage 09 before fitting. Blood uses only Stage 10 unique sequence-supported probe assignments. Brain uses active NCBI lncRNA annotations with the recorded GENCODE crosswalk or explicit NCBI long RNA description. Report the entire accepted feature universe, including filtered and unsuccessful tests. No gene averaging.
- Blood: processed lumi microarray intensity as supplied. Fit statsmodels OLS with intercept, AD indicator, centered age in years, sex and the reported center proxy (three-character sample-description prefix). Rscript/limma unavailable locally. Use chip-clustered sandwich SE with finite-sample correction and t inference on number of chip groups minus one; numeric chip barcode is the title prefix. This accommodates correlated array positions within chip. Residual SD standardizes the coefficient for descriptive comparison only. No moderation or extra normalization. Use the singleton discovery center as a separate fixed intercept. Check rank, residual degrees of freedom and diagnosis-by-category counts for each fit. Age and sex are prespecified irrespective of univariate results.
- Brain: use integer Entrez counts. Complete cases for age and sex, verifying raw ages by GSM. Include age and sex in the negative-binomial design. No batch recorded. Braak stage reflects pathology and adjusting for it would change the target contrast. RIN, cell composition and postmortem interval are unavailable in the saved model metadata. Require at least 10 counts in at least 10 primary samples, independent of diagnosis. Freeze these genes for the sensitivity refit. PyDESeq2 positive-count size factors on eligible lncRNAs, parametric dispersion trend with package fallback, two-sided Wald AD versus Control log2 fold change. This normalization assumes most eligible genes lack a common directional shift. Retain Cook filtering, disable count replacement and independent filtering. Exclude failed gene-wise dispersion, MAP or LFC convergence, invalid SE or nonfinite test results from inference, with reasons. Save normalization, dispersion and influence diagnostics.
- Use complete-case primary samples. Keep every QC-flagged eligible sample. A separate technical stress test excludes concurrent extreme median and IQR flags on arrays, and concurrent extreme library size and high zero fraction with below-median whole-library depth in brain. These signals suggest distorted distributions or low complexity, without establishing technical failure. An isolated high library or extreme intensity is insufficient. Refit covariates and normalization on the sensitivity sample set. Compare coefficients, sign and FDR status. No outcome-dependent exclusions.
- BH separately across valid two-sided tests in each dataset and fit, with strict primary FDR <0.05 and exploratory FDR <0.10. Count blood probe discoveries and distinct genes represented separately. No gene-level combined p-value. Display the lowest-FDR eligible features descriptively. Boxplots show unadjusted values.
- Replication: match the same validated ENSG and identical 50-mer. Report every discovery probe, including unmatched probes. Compare standardized coefficients and all same-gene probe signs. Use discovery sign for the replication one-sided p, BH over all valid matched pairs; classify supporting evidence only for discovery q<0.05, same sign, directional replication q<0.05 and matching primary/sensitivity signs in both cohorts. Opposing signs are inconsistent. Other same-sign pairs are directional only. Untestable/unmatched pairs have insufficient evidence. Same-gene disagreement is disclosed even for supported individual probes. Related cohorts and unresolved patient linkage limit interpretation of replication p-values.
- Cross-tissue comparison: report blood probe and brain gene results by explicit shared ENSG, allowing missing and filtered tests. Compare directions and separate two-sided FDR status. No pooling or effect-size equivalence across tissues.
- Candidate table contains primary q<0.05 records from any unlocked cohort. No full-cohort selection may enter future ML as fixed features. No supervised predictor, co-expression analysis or external outcome access. Deterministic fits, seed 42 where applicable.
